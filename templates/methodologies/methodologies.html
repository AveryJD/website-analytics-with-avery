Methodologies

What's on This Page

This page walks through the methodologies behind some of my projects, including my xG, RAPM, WAR, Elo, and SRS models for my player and team cards. Also included is a glossary of common hockey analytics and data science terms. Click any methodology or the glossary below to expand it.

Everything is built from the NHL's public API: the schedule and standings, play-by-play (shots, goals, faceoffs, penalties, hits, giveaways, and takeaways), shift charts, box scores, and player game logs. It covers the regular seasons from 2009-2010 through 2025-2026. Playoff games aren't part of any model.
