NHL Player Cards

I've created NHL player stat cards that combine advanced analytics, custom models, and clean visuals to highlight player performance. You can explore over 12,300 individual cards of forwards, defensemen, and goalies from the past 17 seasons! The project is mostly complete, but I continue to make occasional improvements to the models and visuals.

NHL Team Cards

I've created NHL team stat cards that combine advanced analytics, custom ratings, and clean visuals to highlight team performance. You can explore over 500 team cards from the past 19 seasons! Like my player cards, this project is mostly complete, but updates are occasionally made.

Other Projects

These projects are less analytics-focused, but are other computer science-related hockey projects.

NHL Schedule Genetic Algorithm

I've built a genetic algorithm that generates optimized NHL regular season schedules, balancing rest, home/away streaks, and travel distance across all 32 teams. It started as my final project for an Evolutionary Computation course, and I've continued developing it since.

View Schedule GA

NHL Interview Analysis

As part of my summer research, I completed a natural language processing project analyzing NHL player and coach interview transcripts to explore the relationship between language and performance. I'm still finalizing the project before publishing the whole thing here.
