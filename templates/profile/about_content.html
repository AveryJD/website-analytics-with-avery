About Analytics With Avery

What is Analytics With Avery?

Analytics With Avery is my personal online space where I showcase my work in sports analytics, with a special focus on hockey. It's a place where I combine my technical skills and love for the game to share projects, visualizations, and analysis that explore the numbers behind the game I love.

On this site, you can find my blog covering research, statistical insights, and whatever else I feel like writing about in hockey, for fans, fellow analysts, and aspiring data scientists. You can also explore my hockey analytics projects, including my NHL player card project, where I evaluate players using various statistics and models.

I also post on multiple social media platforms. On X/Twitter, when hockey news is announced, such as signings, trades, or management changes, I post my player and team cards to highlight it, along with updates on other projects I'm working on and general hockey discussion. On Instagram, I post more casual content, like graphics of hockey top 10 lists. You can check out my socials here.

Whether you're here to browse my latest hockey data projects or blog posts, or to follow my journey in the world of sports analytics, I hope you find something interesting, informative, or just fun to dive into.
