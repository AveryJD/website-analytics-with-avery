NHL Interview Analysis

This project used natural language processing and statistical learning methods to analyze NHL player and coach interview transcripts to explore the relationship between language and performance. It was completed as part of my Undergraduate Research Internship at St. Francis Xavier University during the summer of 2026, under the supervision of Dr. Milton King and Dr. Kyran Cupido.

The research project itself is complete, but I'm still finalizing the write-up, and visuals before publishing them here.
