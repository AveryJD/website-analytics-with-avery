Team Card Explanation

Each ranking shows the team's rank for that attribute (large number, left), the total number of teams (small number, bottom), and a percentile bar showing how many teams they rank above (bar on the right).

Data includes all games played up to and including April 17, 2026, and is from Natural Stat Trick. NHL team logos are the property of the NHL and its teams.

Standings

Record

The team's record (wins-losses-overtime/shootout losses).

Points %

The percentage of available points earned by the team.

Points

Total standings points earned by the team.

Goal Differential

The difference between goals scored and goals allowed (including goals awarded for shootout wins).

Division Rank

The team's rank within its division.

Conference Rank

The team's rank within its conference.

League Rank

The team's overall rank across the league.

5v5 Stats

Goals For %

The percentage of total goals scored by the team at 5v5.

xGoals For %

The percentage of expected goals generated by the team at 5v5.

Shots For %

The percentage of total shots taken by the team at 5v5.

Corsi For %

The percentage of shot attempts (shots + missed + blocked) taken by the team at 5v5.

Fenwick For %

The percentage of unblocked shot attempts (shots + missed) taken by the team at 5v5.

Shooting %

The team's shooting percentage at 5v5 (goals divided by shots).

Save Percentage

The team's save percentage at 5v5 (saves divided by shots).

Elo Graph

Elo Rating

The team's Elo rating over time: a game-by-game rating that rises or falls after each result, with higher values indicating stronger performance at that point in the season. See the Elo ratings write-up on my models page.

SRS Graph

SRS Rank

The team's Simple Rating System (SRS) rank over the past 5 seasons: a season-long strength rating converted into a league-wide rank for visualization. See the SRS model write-up on my models page.

Team Rankings

xGoals For

The team's expected goals generated in all situations, adjusted to a per game rate.

xGoals Against

The team's expected goals allowed in all situations, adjusted to a per game rate.

Goals For

The team's goals scored in all situations, adjusted to a per game rate.

Goals Against

The team's goals allowed in all situations, adjusted to a per game rate.

Power Play

The team's goal-scoring performance at 5v4, adjusted to a per game rate.

Penalty Kill

The team's defensive performance at 4v5, adjusted to a per game rate.

Shots For

The team's shots generated in all situations, adjusted to a per game rate.

Shots Against

The team's shots allowed in all situations, adjusted to a per game rate.

Finishing

The team's ability to convert chances into goals, measured using goals above expected, adjusted to a per game rate.

Goaltending

The team's goaltending performance, measured using goals saved above expected, adjusted to a per game rate.
